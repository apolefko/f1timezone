F1 2026 Schedule FAQ

When does the 2026 F1 season start and end?

The 2026 Formula 1 season opens with the Australian Grand Prix in Melbourne on March 8, 2026 and closes with the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix at Yas Marina on December 6, 2026 — 22 races in total.

What time do F1 races start in US time zones?

It varies by host country. European races typically start around 9 AM Eastern on Sunday mornings, races in the Americas start in the US afternoon, and Asia-Pacific races run overnight. Las Vegas is the exception: a Saturday night race at 11 PM Eastern / 8 PM Pacific. The schedule on this page shows every session converted to your chosen US time zone — and our guide to why F1 start times vary explains the patterns.

Which 2026 races are sprint weekends?

Six rounds run the sprint format in 2026: China, Miami, Canada, Britain, the Netherlands, and Singapore. Sprint weekends add a shorter points-paying race on Saturday plus its own qualifying session on Friday.

What US time zones does F1 Timezone support?

All four continental US time zones: Eastern, Central, Mountain, and Pacific. Times automatically account for daylight saving on each race date.

How can I add the F1 schedule to my calendar?

Download the free .ics season calendar from this page (or a single race from its race page) and open it in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook. Every session appears in your local time with a 30-minute reminder.

Where can I watch F1 in the US in 2026?

Apple TV holds exclusive US broadcast rights from 2026 and streams every session live. Practice sessions and select races are free to watch, practice and qualifying also stream free on Yahoo Sports, and Apple Music carries free live race audio. See our full guide to watching F1 in the US.
